Original Blender findings
Scene gravity: -9.81 m/s². The skirt, draped sleeve and sleeve straps have Blender Cloth modifiers.
The previous FBX exporter removed those modifiers and retained temporary arm/leg attachment weights.
The source .blend is preserved. The Dynamics FBX rebuilds cloth weights on existing bones; it does not change the animation skeleton.

Implementation
M_EX_SurfaceDetail: painted colours retained, scene-lit surfaces, restrained emissive skin fill,
separate cloth/leather/metal roughness, filtered procedural weave normal and roughness detail.
ABP_EX_SecondaryMotion: post-process input pose followed by five KawaiiPhysics groups.
Skirt, sleeve, accessories, hair chains and individual hair locks use different damping/stiffness.
Limb capsules follow animated joints; skirt columns have horizontal constraints and extra collision samples.
Gravity is -980 cm/s². Fixed substepping at 60 Hz, max 4 steps; warm start and teleport reset.
Secondary simulation stops past LOD 2; existing four morph-aware mesh LODs are retained.
This is bone-based cloth/secondary motion, not full per-vertex Chaos cloth and not a Blender cache.
Collision proxies cannot mathematically guarantee that every cloth triangle remains outside the body in every possible pose.
